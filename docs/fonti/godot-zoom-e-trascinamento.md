Exercise for the reader: Perfectionists would instead of the inverse exponential smoothing use a critical
spring, aka. SmoothDamp. It only plays a role when rapidly changing directions, but will subtly feel even
more pleasant.
However, this would have diluted the Gist with a 3rd function and 4th function and at least two more state
variables (especially since Godot does not support passing by reference)
